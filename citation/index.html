What public source means here

Anyone may inspect, fork and propose changes. Canonical changes remain subject to stewardship and versioning principles; public visibility does not turn the Baseline into an open-ended product roadmap.

Reuse

Adaptation is encouraged with attribution

Unless otherwise noted, the written content, canonical questions, practical guidance and portable starter records are available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

What this permits

Organisations and practitioners may share and adapt the authored governance material, including for organisational and commercial use, with appropriate attribution and an indication of changes.

What the licence applies to

The CC BY 4.0 notice applies to the authored governance content. Source code is not separately licensed for reuse unless a file or directory states otherwise.

Versioning

Stable core, evidence-led iteration

The ten questions are canonical for the v1 line. Supporting material can improve without making citations unstable.

v1.x

Clarification, correction or improvement that does not materially change the substance of the ten canonical questions.

v2.0

Considered only if a canonical question is added, removed or materially reframed.

The next practical step

Make uncertainty governable.

Work through the ten questions, then bring unresolved ownership, authority and readiness into a forum that already exists.
