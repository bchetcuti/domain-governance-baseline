Before you begin

A useful review starts with a clear scope.

Ideally, consider the organisation's domain estate rather than just its main website. This is a starting conversation, not an assurance assessment.

01

Think across the estate

Include domains, suppliers and services the organisation owns or depends on. Use the questions to expose where the evidence is incomplete.

02

Answer what you know

Choose Not sure when uncertainty itself needs follow-up. Choose Skip for now only when leaving a question out of this pass.

03

Take the result to work

Copy or print the summary, assign evidence and decisions through an existing forum, and revisit unanswered items. Incomplete reviews are clearly labelled.
