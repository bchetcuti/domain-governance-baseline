Review prompts

  • Is every domain in scope mapped to a business owner and technical operator?
  • Are registrar and DNS access paths named, controlled and recoverable?
  • Are public email-authentication signals reviewed, not merely configured once?
  • Are dependency and incident paths clear enough for a real outage?
  • Are open actions visible to the audience accountable for governance?
This panel records governance visibility. It does not certify control effectiveness or maturity.
