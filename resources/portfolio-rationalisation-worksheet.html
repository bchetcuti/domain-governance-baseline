Retirement checklist

  • The domain has a named business owner approving retirement.
  • Dependent web, email, identity, API and supplier services have been checked.
  • Redirects, customer communications and search implications have been considered.
  • Email sending and inbound mail dependencies have been confirmed.
  • The risk of third-party re-registration and impersonation has been assessed.
  • Retention, redirection or defensive holding has been considered before lapse.
The aim is a deliberate portfolio decision. A domain should not be forgotten into expiry.
