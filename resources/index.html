The method

A 30-minute conversation, not a new programme of work

Bring the reflection summary into a meeting that already needs to happen. Keep attention on uncertainty, accountability and decisions rather than revisiting every technical detail.

5 minutes

Establish scope

Confirm why the review is happening, which domains or services are in scope, and who needs to participate.

10 minutes

Use the ten questions

Start from the baseline reflection. Confirm what can be answered clearly and avoid turning the discussion into a score.

10 minutes

Focus on uncertainty

Discuss the questions marked partial, not in place or not sure. Identify the evidence or authority needed to resolve them.

5 minutes

Assign the outcome

Record decisions, actions, accountable owners and dates. Decide when the domain layer will be revisited.

The outcome

Leave with clarity, not another template to maintain

What was confirmed

The ownership, access, supplier, public-signal and incident answers the group can stand behind.

What remains unknown

The evidence, authority or operational detail still required before the organisation can answer confidently.

What happens next

The decisions, actions, accountable owners, due dates and next review point arising from the conversation.

Use the kit before a board or risk update, after a domain or email incident, during supplier assurance, or as a quarterly domain-governance check-in. It is deliberately generic enough to fit those existing moments without becoming a separate operating model.
